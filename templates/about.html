About us

A library that stays open

Online Library is a digital reading room for students, researchers, and anyone who still believes a book can change an afternoon.

The promise

We gather public-domain literature, open educational texts, and a carefully described catalogue so a reader in Abuja — or anywhere else — can search, read, and keep what is free to keep. The shelves do not close at dusk.

This is an independent project. It is not the National Library of Nigeria, and it is not a university library, though it is built for the kind of questions university students ask.

The collection

Eight disciplines organise the room: Education, Science, Technology, Literature, History, Religion, Computer Science, and Library and Information Science. Classic novels and poems are opening chapters of public-domain works, with a link to the complete text at Project Gutenberg. Study texts signed by the Curriculum Desk are original, and they are complete as published here.

We do not offer downloads of works still in copyright. A catalogue record may describe such a book. It will not pretend to be the book.

How to use the room

  1. Search by title, author, or category.
  2. Open a title and read the first chapter as a guest.
  3. Create a free student account to continue, bookmark, and download legal texts.
  4. Curators add and retire titles from the desk.
